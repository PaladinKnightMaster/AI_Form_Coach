# AI Form Coach - Pricing & Plans Documentation

## Overview

This document outlines the pricing structure, plan features, and monetization strategy for AI Form Coach. Use this for sales conversations, marketing materials, and revenue planning.

---

## Pricing Philosophy

### Core Principles
1. **Value-Based Pricing**: Price reflects the value delivered, not just features
2. **Accessible Entry**: Free tier ensures everyone can experience core value
3. **Clear Upgrade Path**: Obvious value proposition for Pro tier
4. **Fair Usage**: Credit-based AI system ensures fair resource allocation

### Pricing Strategy
- **Freemium Model**: Free tier with core features to drive adoption
- **Subscription Revenue**: Primary revenue stream via Pro subscriptions
- **One-Time Purchases**: Creator Packs for additional revenue
- **Future B2B**: Enterprise pricing for organizations (coming soon)

## Monetization Metrics

### Key Performance Indicators

#### Conversion Metrics
- **Free-to-Pro Conversion Rate**: Target ≥10% (Good), ≥5% (Warning)
- **Pro Trial Rate**: Target ≥10% (Good), ≥5% (Warning)
- **Pro Conversion Rate**: Target ≥20% (Good), ≥10% (Warning)
- **Pack Attach Rate**: Target ≥5% (Good), ≥2% (Warning)

#### Revenue Metrics
- **Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)**: Track monthly subscription revenue
- **Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR)**: Track annual subscription revenue
- **Average Revenue Per User (ARPU)**: Total revenue / active users
- **Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)**: Average revenue per customer over lifetime
- **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)**: Cost to acquire one customer

#### Retention Metrics
- **Monthly Churn Rate**: Target <5% (Good), <10% (Warning)
- **Annual Retention Rate**: Target ≥70% (Good), ≥50% (Warning)
- **Pro Retention Rate**: Target ≥80% (Good), ≥70% (Warning)

### Revenue Projections

#### Assumptions
- Free tier: 90% of users
- Pro tier: 10% of users
- Average Pro subscription: $8/month (or $79/year = $6.58/month average)
- Pack attach rate: 5% of users (Beta 2 release)
- Average pack price: To be determined (Beta 2 release)

#### Calculation Example
- 10,000 users
- 1,000 Pro users (10%)
- Assuming 70% monthly, 30% yearly:
  - Monthly: 700 users × $8 = $5,600/month
  - Yearly: 300 users × $6.58/month = $1,974/month
  - Total MRR: $7,574/month
- ARR: $7,574 × 12 = $90,888/year
- Pack revenue: 500 users × TBD average = TBD (one-time, Beta 2 release)

## FAQ for Sales Teams

### Q: What's included in the free tier?
**A**: Free tier includes AI form coaching, basic nutrition tracking, featured workout plans, health monitoring, and core platform features. Limited to 10 recent sessions in history.

### Q: Why should users upgrade to Pro?
**A**: Pro unlocks personalized AI workout plans, advanced nutrition features, unlimited sessions, leaderboards, monthly challenges, data export, and priority support.

### Q: What's the difference between monthly and yearly Pro?
**A**: Yearly Pro offers the same features as monthly but at a discounted annual rate, saving users money while providing the same value.

### Q: What are Creator Packs?
**A**: Creator Packs are premium workout programs and specialized training content created by fitness experts. They're one-time purchases that provide lifetime access to expert-created content.

### Q: Is there a refund policy?
**A**: Refund policy is planned for Beta 2 release. Currently, refunds are handled on a case-by-case basis. Please contact support for refund requests. The formal refund policy will be implemented in Beta 2 and will be based on payment processor capabilities and legal requirements.

### Q: Can users cancel anytime?
**A**: Yes, users can cancel their Pro subscription anytime. They'll retain access until the end of their billing period.

### Q: What happens to data if users cancel?
**A**: Users can export their data before canceling. After cancellation, they'll have access to free tier features and their historical data.
