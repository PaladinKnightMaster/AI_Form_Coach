## 📊 Key Metrics Summary

### Engagement & Retention
- **D1 Retention**: ≥40% (Good), ≥20% (Warning)
- **W1 Retention**: ≥20% (Good), ≥10% (Warning)
- **Session Completion**: ≥80% (Good), ≥60% (Warning)
- **Monthly Churn**: <5% (Good), <10% (Warning)

### Quality
- **Verification Rate**: ≥70% (Good), ≥50% (Warning)
- **Median FPS**: ≥30 (Good), ≥20 (Warning)
- **Visibility Rate**: ≥80% (Good), ≥60% (Warning)
- **Average Confidence**: ≥80% (Good), ≥60% (Warning)

### Monetization
- **Pro Trial Rate**: ≥10% (Good), ≥5% (Warning)
- **Pro Conversion Rate**: ≥20% (Good), ≥10% (Warning)
- **Pack Attach Rate**: ≥5% (Good), ≥2% (Warning)
- **LTV/CAC Ratio**: ≥3:1 (Good)

### Operations
- **Device Processing**: ≥95% (Good)
- **API Latency**: ≤150ms (Good)
- **Storage per User**: ≤50MB (Good)
- **Support Tickets**: <2% of users (Good)

## 🎯 Core Value Propositions

### Primary Value Proposition
> **Train smarter, not harder.** Get professional-grade form coaching, personalized nutrition tracking, and AI-powered workout plans—all in one privacy-first platform that works entirely on your device.

### Key Messages
1. **Privacy-First**: "Your privacy is protected - we process everything on your device"
2. **Professional-Grade**: "Get biomechanical analysis that would cost $200+ per session"
3. **All-in-One**: "One app for everything - coaching, nutrition, planning, health"
4. **Real-Time**: "Get instant feedback as you exercise - not after the workout"
5. **Comprehensive**: "Complete fitness ecosystem with integrated features"
