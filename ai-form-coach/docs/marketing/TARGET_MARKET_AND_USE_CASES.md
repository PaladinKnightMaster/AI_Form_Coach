# AI Form Coach - Target Market & Use Cases

## Overview

This document outlines the target markets, user personas, and use cases for AI Form Coach. Use this for sales targeting, marketing segmentation, and product development prioritization.

---

## Target Market Segments

### 1. Individual Fitness Enthusiasts (B2C - Primary)

#### Demographics
- **Age**: 18-45 years old
- **Income**: Middle to upper-middle class
- **Location**: Urban and suburban areas
- **Education**: High school to college educated
- **Technology**: Smartphone users, comfortable with apps

#### Psychographics
- **Fitness Goals**: General fitness, weight loss, muscle gain, athletic performance
- **Motivation**: Health improvement, appearance, performance, competition
- **Pain Points**: 
  - Lack of access to personal trainers
  - Uncertainty about proper form
  - Difficulty tracking nutrition and workouts together
  - Privacy concerns with fitness apps
  - Cost of personal training
- **Values**: Health, privacy, value, convenience, results

#### Use Cases

##### Use Case 1: Home Workout Enthusiast
**Scenario**: User works out at home, wants professional form coaching without hiring a trainer

**Pain Points**:
- No access to personal trainer at home
- Uncertain about proper form
- Worried about injury from bad form
- Can't afford regular personal training sessions

**How AI Form Coach Solves**:
- Real-time form coaching at home
- Professional-grade 3D biomechanical analysis
- Injury prevention alerts
- Cost-effective (5% of personal trainer cost)

**Value Proposition**: "Get professional form coaching at home for a fraction of the cost"

---

##### Use Case 2: Nutrition & Fitness Tracker
**Scenario**: User wants to track both workouts and nutrition in one place

**Pain Points**:
- Using multiple apps (one for workouts, one for nutrition)
- Data doesn't integrate between apps
- Manual food entry is time-consuming
- No connection between nutrition and workout performance

**How AI Form Coach Solves**:
- All-in-one platform (workouts + nutrition)
- Integrated data and insights
- AI food recognition (quick logging)
- Nutrition insights connected to workout performance

**Value Proposition**: "One app for everything - workouts, nutrition, and planning"

---

##### Use Case 3: Competitive Fitness Enthusiast
**Scenario**: User wants to compete on leaderboards and track progress

**Pain Points**:
- Wants fair competition
- Needs accurate progress tracking
- Wants to see improvement over time
- Needs motivation through competition

**How AI Form Coach Solves**:
- Verified-only leaderboards (fair competition)
- Comprehensive progress tracking
- Monthly challenges
- Detailed analytics and trends

**Value Proposition**: "Fair competition with verified leaderboards and comprehensive tracking"

---

##### Use Case 4: Privacy-Conscious User
**Scenario**: User wants fitness tracking but is concerned about privacy

**Pain Points**:
- Doesn't want video sent to servers
- Concerned about data breaches
- Wants control over personal data
- Needs GDPR/CCPA compliance

**How AI Form Coach Solves**:
- On-device processing (video never leaves device)
- Privacy-first architecture
- Complete user data control
- GDPR/CCPA compliant by design

**Value Proposition**: "Complete privacy protection - we process everything on your device"

## Market Size & Opportunity

#### B2C Market
- **Global Fitness App Market**: $4.4B (2023), projected $15.6B by 2030
- **Online Fitness Market**: $59.2B (2023), growing at 33.1% CAGR
- **Target Segment**: 10-20% of fitness app users = $440M-$880M (2023)

#### B2B Market
- **Corporate Wellness Market**: $61B (2023), growing at 7.1% CAGR
- **Healthcare/Rehabilitation Market**: $8.5B (2023), growing at 6.2% CAGR
- **Target Segment**: 5-10% of market = $3.5B-$6.1B (2023)

### Serviceable Addressable Market (SAM)

#### B2C Segment
- **Target**: Fitness enthusiasts who want form coaching
- **Size**: 50-100M potential users globally
- **Penetration**: 1-5% = 500K-5M users
- **Revenue Potential**: $50M-$500M ARR (at $10/month ARPU)

#### B2B Segment
- **Target**: Fitness professionals, corporate wellness, healthcare
- **Size**: 100K-500K potential organizations globally
- **Penetration**: 1-5% = 1K-25K organizations
- **Revenue Potential**: $10M-$250M ARR (at $1K-$10K per organization/year)

### Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM)

#### Year 1 Targets
- **B2C**: 10K-50K users = $1M-$5M ARR
- **B2B**: 10-100 organizations = $100K-$1M ARR
- **Total**: $1.1M-$6M ARR

- **B2C**: 100K-500K users = $10M-$50M ARR
